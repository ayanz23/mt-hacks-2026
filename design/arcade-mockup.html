About the event

MT-haCks is a one-day hackathon hosted by the Muslim Tech Collaborative (MTC) at Rutgers. Students come together to design and build projects that solve a real problem for a real person or community.

Whether this is your first hackathon or your tenth, you're welcome here. Come with a team or come alone. We'll match solo hackers with teammates at the start of the day.

Hack for impact

We're looking for projects that work, not just demos. Think about who you're building for and what would actually make their day better.

How to play

Frequently asked questions
What is a hackathon?

An event where people team up to build a project, such as an app, website or hardware hack, in a short amount of time. No prior experience is needed.

When and where is it?

Saturday, November 21, 2026, 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM, at Rutgers University, New Brunswick. We'll share the exact location soon.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. MT-haCks is free to attend.

How big can teams be?

Teams can have 1–4 people. If you don't have a team, register anyway and we'll match you with one at the start of the event.

Do I need to be 18?

Participants must be 18 or older. If you're under 18, contact the organizers for a guardian consent form.

Will there be food?

Yes. Tell us about any dietary restrictions or allergies on the registration form so we can plan for you.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and anything else you need to build your project.

I have another question.

Email us at rutgersmtc@gmail.com.
